Introduction

At Famous Jelly Luxe (FJL), your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website and interact with our services.

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including:

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information:

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information only:

Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@fjl.com with your request.
