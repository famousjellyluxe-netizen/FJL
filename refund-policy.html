Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Overview

At Famous Jelly Luxe, we are committed to ensuring customer satisfaction with every purchase. This Refund Policy outlines our procedures for handling refunds and returns.

Important Note: All FJL sales are final. We do not accept returns, exchanges, or cancellations on any products. This is due to the exclusive nature of our limited releases and special designs.

When Refunds May Apply

The following are the only circumstances under which a refund may be processed:

1. Defective Products

If you receive a product with manufacturing defects:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  • Provide your order number and photos of the defect
  • Email: support@fjl.com
  • We will review and work with you on a resolution

2. Incorrect Item Shipped

If we ship you an item different from what was ordered:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  • Include your order number and photos
  • We will arrange for the correct item to be shipped or process a refund

3. Payment Processing Errors

If you were double-charged or charged in error:

  • We will identify and reverse the erroneous charge
  • Refunds typically process within 5-10 business days

Refund Process

When a refund is approved, the following process will be followed:

  1. We will notify you via email with refund approval details
  2. If the item needs to be returned, we will provide a return shipping label
  3. Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will process your refund
  4. Refund will be credited to your original payment method
  5. Allow 5-10 business days for the refund to appear in your account

What Is NOT Refundable

The following are NOT eligible for refunds:

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items without original tags or packaging
  • Items damaged due to customer misuse
  • Items purchased during final sale or clearance
  • Shipping costs (unless the error was on our part)

Unauthorized Charges

If you believe your credit card was charged without authorization:

  • Contact us immediately at support@fjl.com
  • Provide your order number and any relevant details
  • We will investigate and resolve the issue promptly
  • You may also dispute the charge with your credit card company

Cancellations

Orders cannot be cancelled once they have been placed. If you need to cancel an order:

  • Contact us immediately at support@fjl.com
  • If the order has not been processed, we will attempt to cancel it
  • If processing has begun, cancellation may not be possible
  • Once shipped, the order cannot be cancelled, but you may refuse delivery

Gift Purchases

Items purchased as gifts are subject to the same refund policy. The gift recipient should contact us with the order information within 7 days if there is an issue with the product.

Contact Us

For questions about our Refund Policy or to initiate a refund request, please contact us:

Email: support@fjl.com
Address: Famous Jelly Luxe, [Your Address]
Phone: [Your Phone Number]
Response Time: We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2 business days

Policy Changes

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting to our website. Your continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
